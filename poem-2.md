Two roads diverged in splendid wood, 
And both looked very bright and good. 

I chose to go on the one most traveled on,
And smiled to see the golden Dawn. 

With friends beside me and skies of blue, 
Every adventure feels to be brand new. 

The path was decades, the journey sweet, 
With Joy in every step and beat.

Even ages and ages hence,
the grass is green from on the fence.
