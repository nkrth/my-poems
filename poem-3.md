Passing by the City on a Summer's Morning,
With golden light, the busy street adorning.

I have seen many a hunn'it miles without sleep, 
Enough to know the story's shallow deep.

With joyous steps where sunlit paths unfold, 
I share in stories that awaited to be retold.

A caller rings, I put them on hold,
I must not wimper for they think I am bold.

Promise me this, promise me that.
All these promises made me fat.
Atleast I am not judged by the cat.
She knows how long here I have sat.

Dwell too long, dwell too less,
another cuppa tea might bless
Another walk outside de-stressed,
May help along this guy's digest.

The skies themselves are of varied mood.
All anyone asks is "Who is this Dude?"

Heart on sleeve with a scheme to daydream,
is a soul capturing the sunset's beams
and carrying forward like a meme,
a hope that one does profit Team.

I have pushed a rhyme, but not to prod. 
Lost track of time, just like gentle Zod.
At breakneck speed, the world goes on.
So, I take an endless break on the lawn. 

Under a tree might never last, 
as that chill moment is now in the past, 
Searching for newer destiny
but responses default to "don't pester me."

Existence in this moment and the next, 
is not endless but may it live on in text.

A good night is said and dusted off, 
to have dreams as old as Dave Hasselhoff.

Beep beep, Coyote.
