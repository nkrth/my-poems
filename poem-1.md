Once upon a morning cheery, 
while I pondered, bright and clearly,
Over many a lovely layout, feeling energetic, oh so dearly --

While I chatted, softly sipping,
suddenly there came a flipping,
As of little pages skipping, landing right upon my floor.

"'Tis a story", I gently whispered, 
"dropping right upon my floor." 

Only this and nothing more.
