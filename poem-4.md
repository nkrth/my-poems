The room aches in astounding agony
the walls hold the weight of a thousand sighs

A sliver of light reaches in from the window
it stays there and finds a patch of floor to call its own.
The wood begins to warm under that gold touch.
The silence in the corner begins to soften. 

The light carves a golden path, 
leading straight to the centre of the room
The light warms and the room begins to soften
and the agony finally lets go and begins to fade.

The silence becomes a song.
The shadows slowly step back.
The room finally exhales.
The air feels light again

The morning air drifts in smelling of rain and possibility.
Nothing but the quiet gold glow of the afternoon.
The dust begins to dance in the beams.
